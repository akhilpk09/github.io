Security & Privacy

My Spend provides security-focused features for protecting access to your financial records and backups.

🔒 App Lock

Protect access to the application using a 4–6 digit PIN and supported biometric authentication.

🔐 Encrypted Backups

Backup functionality supports AES-256-GCM encryption with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256-based key derivation.

☁️ Google Drive Backup

Optional Google Drive backup and restore allows users to keep encrypted My Spend backup data in their selected Google Drive account.

📱 Local Data

My Spend is designed primarily for personal financial record management on the user's device.
