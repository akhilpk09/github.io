My Spend - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

My Spend ("the App") is a personal finance and expense management application designed to help users record and manage their personal financial information, including income, expenses, transfers, credit cards, EMIs and related financial records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the My Spend application.

1. Information You Enter

My Spend may allow you to enter and store information such as:

  • Income and expense records
  • Transaction descriptions
  • Categories
  • Bank and payment account information entered by you
  • Credit card information entered by you
  • Credit card billing and due dates
  • EMI information
  • Transfer records
  • Refund information
  • Financial notes and related records

This information is used only to provide the financial management features of the App.

2. SMS Permission

My Spend may request permission to read SMS messages when you choose to use automatic transaction detection and SMS-based income and expense import features.

SMS access is used to identify relevant financial transaction information, such as bank or payment transaction messages, so that supported transactions can be suggested or imported into the App.

SMS access is permission-based. You can deny or revoke SMS permission through your Android device settings. Features that require SMS access may not work when the permission is denied.

My Spend does not use SMS access for advertising purposes.

3. Financial Information

Financial information entered or imported into My Spend is used to provide features such as expense tracking, income tracking, account management, credit-card tracking, EMI management, reports and related calculations.

My Spend does not sell your personal financial information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Local Storage

My Spend may store application data locally on your Android device to provide offline functionality and preserve your financial records.

Locally stored information remains on your device unless you choose to export, back up, restore or otherwise transfer the information using features provided by the App.

5. Backup and Restore

My Spend may provide local backup and restore functionality. Backup files may contain information stored in the App.

Where encrypted backup functionality is provided, backup data may be protected using encryption before it is stored or transferred.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for keeping backup files and authentication credentials secure.

6. Google Drive Backup

If you choose to use Google Drive backup or restore, the App may interact with your Google Drive account to upload or restore your selected backup data.

Google Drive access is initiated by you and is used only for the backup and restore functionality provided by the App.

Google Drive services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

7. PIN and Biometric Protection

My Spend may provide PIN and biometric authentication features to help protect access to the App.

Biometric authentication is handled through the security mechanisms provided by your Android device. My Spend does not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric data.

8. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, My Spend may request Android permissions such as:

  • SMS permission for supported automatic transaction detection
  • Notification-related permission where required for reminders
  • Storage or file access where required for backup, restore or statement files
  • Internet access for online services such as cloud backup

Permissions are requested only when required for the corresponding feature.

9. Third-Party Services

Certain features may use third-party services, such as Google services, for authentication, cloud storage or other functionality.

Third-party services operate according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Sharing

My Spend does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a third-party service only when necessary for a feature that you explicitly use, such as cloud backup or restore.

11. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures may be used to help protect information handled by the App, including local protection mechanisms and encrypted backup functionality where supported.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally in My Spend can generally be removed by deleting the relevant records or uninstalling the App, subject to Android system behavior and any backups you have created.

Backup copies stored by you, including cloud backups, may remain available until you delete them from the relevant storage service.

13. Children's Privacy

My Spend is intended for personal financial management and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of My Spend, please contact the developer through the contact information provided with the application or its official website.
