Job Sheet – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, accessed, stored, processed, and protected when you use the Job Sheet application.

1. About Job Sheet

Job Sheet is a business management application designed to help businesses manage job sheets, customers, technicians, items, stock, purchases, suppliers, debit notes, invoices, and completed work.

The application may also provide features such as phone calling, WhatsApp communication, printing, authentication, and cloud-based data storage.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Job Sheet may process different types of information that you provide directly or that is generated through your use of the application.

2.1 Account Information

When you create or access an account, the application may process information required for authentication and account management.

This may include information such as your mobile phone number, authentication information, business information, and account identifiers.

2.2 Business Information

You may provide business-related information such as business name, contact details, address, and other information necessary to configure and use the application.

2.3 Customer Information

Job Sheet may allow you to store customer information required for managing jobs and communicating with customers.

This may include information such as:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Job details
  • Service or repair information
  • Invoice-related information
  • Other information entered by the business user

2.4 Technician Information

The application may allow businesses to maintain technician records.

Such information may include technician names, contact information, job assignments, and other information entered by the business user.

2.5 Job Information

Job Sheet may store information associated with jobs created by users.

This may include job descriptions, customer details, job status, dates, items, services, technician assignments, amounts, notes, and other information entered by the user.

2.6 Item and Stock Information

The application may process item and inventory information such as item names, quantities, prices, stock levels, purchase information, and other inventory-related records.

2.7 Purchase and Supplier Information

Job Sheet may store purchase records and supplier information entered by users.

Such information may include supplier names, contact information, purchase details, quantities, prices, dates, and related records.

2.8 Invoice and Debit Note Information

The application may process information required to create and maintain invoices, debit notes, and related business documents.

This may include customer information, item details, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, totals, dates, and other information entered by the user.

3. Authentication

Job Sheet may use authentication services to identify authorized users and protect access to business accounts and data.

Authentication may involve mobile phone numbers, OTP verification, account identifiers, or other authentication mechanisms supported by the application.

Authentication information is used to provide secure access to the relevant account and associated application features.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through Job Sheet may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing job sheet management features.
  • Managing customer and technician records.
  • Managing items and stock.
  • Managing purchase and supplier records.
  • Creating and maintaining invoices and debit notes.
  • Maintaining completed-work records.
  • Synchronizing supported business data between devices and cloud services.
  • Providing application security and authentication.
  • Improving application reliability and functionality.
  • Responding to support requests and technical issues.
  • Maintaining and protecting the application's services.

5. Firebase and Cloud Firestore

Job Sheet may use Firebase and Google Cloud Firestore services for authentication, cloud data storage, synchronization, and other application functionality.

Depending on the features used, information associated with your account and business may be stored or processed through these services.

Firebase and Google Cloud services may process technical and account information according to their applicable policies and service requirements.

6. Business Data Separation

Job Sheet is designed to associate business information with the relevant authenticated account or business context.

Access to cloud-stored business information may be controlled using authentication and application security mechanisms.

Users should not share their authentication credentials, OTPs, passwords, or account access with unauthorized persons.

7. Phone and Calling Features

Job Sheet may allow users to initiate phone calls using telephone numbers stored in customer or business records.

The application may use the device's calling functionality to initiate a call.

Job Sheet does not control the telephone network or mobile carrier. Any applicable call or network charges are the responsibility of the user.

8. WhatsApp

Job Sheet may allow users to open WhatsApp or use WhatsApp-related functionality for communicating with customers.

For example, users may use WhatsApp to send job-related reminders, status information, or other business messages.

Job Sheet does not control WhatsApp's services or the processing of information by WhatsApp.

Your use of WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's applicable privacy policy and terms.

9. Permissions

Depending on the features available in the application, Job Sheet may request certain device permissions.

These permissions may be required to provide specific functionality such as accessing the internet, making phone calls, sending or opening messages, accessing device storage, printing, or other application functions.

Permissions are used only to support the relevant functionality of the application.

10. Data Storage

Job Sheet may store information locally on the user's device and, where applicable, in cloud services used by the application.

The type and location of storage may depend on the features being used and the configuration of the application.

Users should maintain appropriate backups of important business information.

11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information processed through Job Sheet.

These measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, secure cloud infrastructure, and application-level security mechanisms.

However, no electronic storage system, internet transmission, or software application can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Sharing

Job Sheet does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate application features, provide authentication, store data, maintain cloud services, or provide technical infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental or regulatory request.

13. Third-Party Services

Job Sheet may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These services may include:

  • Authentication services

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested application functionality, maintain business records, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or protect legitimate interests.
